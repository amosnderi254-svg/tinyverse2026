How We Design

Our Process

Research before pixels. Every interface decision is grounded in evidence from real users, not assumptions from a brief.

01
Step One
Discovery & Research

User interviews, competitive benchmarking, heuristic audits — we map what your users actually need before proposing a single solution.

02
Step Two
Information Architecture

Sitemaps, user flows, and content hierarchies built so every screen has one clear purpose and every path leads somewhere useful.

03
Step Three
Prototype & Test

Interactive prototypes validated with real users before a line of production code is written — so failures cost a day, not a sprint.

04
Step Four
Deliver & Iterate

Annotated design files, component libraries, developer handoff, and a post-launch review cycle — the work isn't done when we hand it over.

Our Design Principles

Six Pillars of Intentional Design

Good design is invisible. Great design makes the right action feel obvious. These are the disciplines that get us there.

Empathy
User Research

Interviews, surveys, and usability testing grounded in what real users say, do, and avoid — not what stakeholders assume about them.

Structure
Journey Mapping

We chart every step a user takes from discovery to action, finding the friction points that cost you conversions before they're built in.

Speed
Rapid Prototyping

Clickable prototypes in Figma within days — fast enough to test assumptions early, refined enough to hand off to development with confidence.

Inclusion
Accessibility First

WCAG 2.1 AA as a baseline, not an afterthought. Colour contrast, screen reader structure, and keyboard navigation baked into every component.

Scale
Design Systems

Tokens, components, and documentation that give your team a shared language — so every new page ships faster and looks like it belongs.

Evidence
Data-Driven Iteration

Post-launch analytics, heatmaps, and session recordings feed back into design. We treat launch as the beginning of the research cycle, not the end.
