Case Study

WooCommerce + M-Pesa
done properly.

A Nairobi fashion retailer needed a checkout flow that matched how Kenyans actually pay. Here's what we shipped and what moved.

⚡ WooCommerce Build

From abandoned carts to completed orders , same products, new checkout.

The client was running a WooCommerce store on shared hosting with a generic M-Pesa plugin that had no callback validation. Customers would pay, the payment would register with Safaricom, but the order would stay "pending" , and the client would manually process them at EOD.

We rebuilt the checkout on a VPS with proper Daraja STK Push integration: callback endpoint with HMAC signature validation, idempotency keys to prevent double-processing, and a real-time order status webhook that updated the customer's screen within 8 seconds of payment confirmation.
